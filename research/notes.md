# Source notes

- **Report:** “SaaS platform that turns private work into verifiable proof for NDA-bound developers,” IdeaBrowser Idea of the Day, 2026-11-02. Public URL: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **Headline economics:** IB Index 7.5; $2M–$4M ARR ceiling; moderate difficulty; $25K–$50K capital; first revenue in 60–90 days; year-one estimate $80K–$180K.
- **Buyer:** NDA-bound experienced developers plus recruiters, staffing firms, and technical hiring teams that distrust AI-assisted resumes.
- **Pain/status quo:** candidates either omit their strongest work, describe it weakly, risk breaching an NDA, or take another assessment. Recruiters verify tests and employment, not authorship of private work.
- **Wedge/gap:** local-first contribution extraction plus a human oral defense. Only counts, ranges, and signed evidence leave the device; code remains local.
- **Why now:** the report cites GitHub Octoverse 2025 saying 81.5% of contributions were private, GoodTime’s 500+ TA-leader survey ranking fraudulent/AI-assisted candidates the top 2026 challenge, and Gartner’s forecast that one in four candidates will be fake by 2028.
- **Competitors:** Workproof and DevClocked (git-history proof); Karat ($200–$450/interview cited); HackerRank ($1,990/yr Starter, $4,490/yr Pro cited); CodeSignal; Credly; Cluely; Andela/Woven Teams. The moat cannot be a CLI alone.
- **Demand signals:** nine reported pain receipts, including Teamblind NDA complaints; Fabric’s 19,368-interview analysis reportedly flagged 38.5% for AI assistance. Solution-keyword volume is reportedly zero; category searches are only 170–720/month.
- **Offer ladder:** free local CLI; $99 human-certified credential ($49 annual renewal); $149/month or $1,490/year recruiter seat; $499/month staffing team plan.
- **GTM:** sold-not-searched. Start in Teamblind, r/ExperiencedDevs, r/cscareerquestions, and TA-leader communities; cold-contact 800 TA leaders in the report’s 90-day model.
- **Risks:** recruiter cold start, employer/IP policy violations, weak credential trust, adverse selection, reviewer quality, and incumbents copying the feature.
- **Build direction:** validate a consented proof-pack workflow before implementing repository access. Keep extraction local, define evidence boundaries, and make oral-defense review auditable.
- **Cited domains/organizations:** github.com, jetbrains.com, Gartner/CNBC, GoodTime, Greenhouse, Fabric/HeroHunt, Teamblind, Reddit, HackerRank, Karat, Credly, Andela, Workproof, DevClocked.
