# Office Hours diagnostic

1. **Specific customer:** senior engineers with strong private-repository work who are entering an active job search, and technical staffing agencies placing them into $150K+ roles.
2. **Painful status quo:** a candidate cannot show the work; the recruiter cannot distinguish genuine ownership from polished AI claims; both fall back to another test that senior candidates resent.
3. **Desperate use case:** a finalist must prove ownership of a private system within 72 hours without sharing code or breaching employer policy.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** a $99, candidate-consented proof pack: local evidence summary, a 20-minute expert oral defense, explicit limitations, and a 30-day recruiter link. Do not start with an ATS platform.
5. **Observation plan:** recruit 10 job-seeking senior engineers and watch them complete a manual local extraction; record every field they refuse to expose. Then put three packs in front of five TA leaders and ask them to rank candidates before and after viewing.
6. **Validation:** success is five completed packs, three recruiter requests for another candidate, and at least two paid $99 defenses. “Interesting” is not evidence.
7. **Future-fit:** if AI makes tests less trusted and work stays private, evidence networks matter more. If employers prohibit extraction or incumbents add credible work verification, become the neutral oral-defense protocol or stop.
