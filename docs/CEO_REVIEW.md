# CEO review

## Challenge
The premise overstates software leverage. Workproof already demonstrates local extraction, while Karat and HackerRank own recruiter trust. Zero solution-keyword volume means this is a two-sided credibility business disguised as SaaS. A new score may add noise, and touching employer repositories can create catastrophic legal exposure. The $2M–$4M ceiling is an estimate, not evidence.

The original wedge also asked recruiters to trust a credential before candidates wanted it. GTM, reviewer supply, appeals, false attribution, disparate impact, and outcome measurement were under-specified. A CLI is copyable; “AI scoring” would worsen the trust problem. Static hosting cannot imply a live backend.

## Decisions after review
- Candidate pays first; recruiters are design partners until repeat requests exist.
- The product proves narrow evidence boundaries, not generalized skill or employability.
- The prototype makes consent mandatory, labels all fixtures, uses deterministic logic, and says Python is not deployed.
- Production extraction stays local and allow-listed; employer policy and legal review are gates.
- Moat thesis moves from parsing to calibrated human defense, evidence integrity, and accepted credentials.
- Kill the project if 10 concierge packs yield fewer than two $99 purchases or five TA leaders produce zero repeat requests.
